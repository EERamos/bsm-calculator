Greeks: Risk Sensitivities

Delta$\partial V / \partial S$ Rate of change of the option value w.r.t. the underlying. The hedge ratio.
Gamma$\partial^2 V / \partial S^2$ Convexity of the option value. Peaks at-the-money and decays as expiry approaches for OTM/ITM.
Vega$\partial V / \partial \sigma$ Sensitivity to volatility. Long options are long vega; positive for both calls and puts.
Theta$\partial V / \partial t$ Time decay. Almost always negative for long options; quantifies the "rent" you pay for optionality.
Rho$\partial V / \partial r$ Sensitivity to the risk-free rate. Matters most for long-dated options.
Vanna$\partial^2 V / \partial S \partial \sigma$ Cross-sensitivity. Critical for delta hedging when vol is itself moving.
Volga$\partial^2 V / \partial \sigma^2$ Convexity in volatility. Drives the cost of vol-of-vol exposure (vol smile/skew dynamics).
Charm$\partial^2 V / \partial S \partial t$ Delta decay. Important for overnight gap risk on positions held into expiry.
